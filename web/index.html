How RELOOP Works

Experience frictionless sustainability in four straightforward steps.

STAGE 01
♻️

Bring Recyclables

Gather plastic bottles, aluminium cans, or clean cardboard and visit your nearest supermarket station.

STAGE 02
⚖️

Operator Inspection

Our certified collection operator weighs or counts items and records them in the RELOOP station portal.

STAGE 03
🪙

Earn Reward Points

The Java points engine immediately verifies the submission and credits your digital wallet.

STAGE 04
🎁

Redeem & Repeat

Use accumulated points to unlock discounts and coupons from partnered grocery stores and cafes.
